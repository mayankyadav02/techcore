About

Technology should solve business problems, not create new ones.

TechCore is a technology practice for organisations that need websites, applications, and operational software they can actually run. We listen first, then design systems around how the work happens.

Who we are: a delivery team spanning product engineering, design, cloud, and assurance. Why it matters: growth stalls when digital work is treated as decoration. We stay until the first release is usable — and document how the next one should land.

Why TechCore

What you should expect from us.

01

02

Discovery, design, engineering, and launch sit in one practice so the artefact that ships is the one that was promised.

03

Modern, maintainable technology

Transparent process

Process

How a programme typically runs.

From the first conversation through support after launch.

  1. 01

    Step 01

    Discover

    Understand the business problem, constraints, and the smallest outcome that would prove value.

  2. 02

    Step 02

    Plan

    Sequence work into releases a sponsor can recognise, with risks written down before build starts.

  3. 03

    Step 03

    Design

    Define structure, access, and interface so engineering implements a system — not a pile of screens.

  4. 04

    Step 04

    Develop

    Build in vertical slices against agreed contracts, with review as a habit rather than a ceremony.

  5. 05

    Step 05

    Test

    Prove the journeys that matter, including exceptions, on environments that resemble production.

  6. 06

    Step 06

    Launch

    Release with a rollback path, monitoring, and a named owner on both sides of the handover.

  7. 07

    Step 07

    Support

    Stabilise, train operators, and agree how change will enter the system after go-live.

FAQ

Questions we expect.

Is TechCore a real company?+−
TechCore is a fictional firm used to demonstrate a complete services website and CMS. Content, case studies, and quotations on this site are illustrative, not claims about live client contracts.
What kind of work do you take on?+−
Websites, web and mobile applications, custom operational software, applied AI where it has a baseline, cloud delivery, product design, security engineering, and advisory that can turn into a build.
How do engagements typically start?+−
With a short conversation and, where useful, a written discovery. Build work begins when the decision and the first release are clear enough to estimate honestly.
Do you work as an embedded team or as a vendor?+−
Either. Programmes are scoped so your team can take ownership, or we can remain as the delivery unit under a named lead.
Which industries do you take on?+−
We structure work around healthcare, education, retail, finance, manufacturing, property, logistics, and early-stage product companies — always as a technology partner, not a domain operator.
How long does a typical project take?+−
A focused website can complete in weeks. Product and operations platforms usually run in staged releases over months. Timelines are agreed after we understand constraints, not before.
What do Launch, Scale, and Enterprise mean?+−
They describe engagement shape, not a public price list. Launch is a focused first presence. Scale is a working digital product. Enterprise is operational software and multi-team programmes. Every package uses a Custom Quote.
Do you publish rates or package fees?+−
No. Fees depend on scope, integrations, and risk. Use Start a Project for a Custom Quote. Any numbers you see elsewhere on the web are not offers from this demonstration site.
Do you provide support after launch?+−
Yes, when it is scoped. Support is a continuation of the same engineering practice: monitoring, small changes, and a written path for larger work.
Can you work with our existing tools and cloud account?+−
Yes. We typically integrate with systems of record and operate in the client tenancy with least-privilege access rather than forcing a greenfield stack.
Are the case studies and testimonials real clients?+−
No. ShopFlow, CarePlus, FleetPro, LearnHub, EstatePro, FinServe, GymCore, and FoodHub are fictional programmes. Testimonials are labelled fictional and illustrate tone, not named results.
How should we start a conversation?+−
Use Start a Project for a scoped brief, or Contact if you want a conversation first. A valid submission is stored for the TechCore team on this demonstration site.

Have a project in mind?

Share a short brief. We work discovery-first, treat security as default, and will say honestly whether TechCore is the right team — then what a first release could look like.
